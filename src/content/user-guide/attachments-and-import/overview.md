This section is about getting files into Myne and keeping track of them. Attachments are files of any type — images, PDFs, video, audio and documents, but also any other file you drop in — stored inside the encrypted vault; voice notes are audio you record straight into a note; the attachments library is where you see and manage them all; the importer is a one-time way to bring an existing vault across, whether it came from another app or from a backup of another Myne vault; and the browser clipper saves web pages into your vault from a browser extension.

## What's in this section

- **[Attachments](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/attachments/)**: adding files to notes — images, PDFs, video, audio, and text and spreadsheet files all render in place; anything else is kept as an export-only card — the size limits, and how they're stored.
- **[Voice notes](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/voice-notes/)**: recording audio directly into a note, where it lives as an encrypted attachment.
- **[The attachments library](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/attachments-library/)**: browsing, searching, and managing every file in the vault.
- **[Import your notes](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/import-your-notes/)**: bringing an existing vault into Myne. Obsidian `.base` files (saved views) are brought in separately — see [Saved views](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/saved-views/).
- **[Combine two vaults](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/combine-two-vaults/)**: bringing the notes from one Myne vault into another. It is the highest-fidelity source, since both sides speak the same format: links between the notes you bring, the attachments those notes use, and your added dictionary words all come across. Some things still stay behind, and the article says which.
- **[Browser clipper](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/browser-clipper/)**: saving a web page or selection into your vault as a note with the Myne Clipper extension, and managing the browsers you have paired.
