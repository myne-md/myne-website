The Myne name, the Myne "M" mark, the wordmark and the app icon (together, the
**Marks**) are **not** covered by this repository's licences. The code is MIT and
the prose is CC-BY-4.0 (see [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md)); the Marks are
neither. They are common-law trademarks asserted through use.

## What the licences do and do not give you

MIT grants you the **code**: use, study, modify and redistribute it. CC-BY-4.0
grants you the **prose**, with attribution. Neither grants anything about the
**name**. A fork that presents itself as Myne, or a product named
`Myne Something`, is using the Marks without permission even though its code is
lawfully derived.

A fork must use its own name and marks. Referential use needs no permission:
an article, a review, a tutorial, a comparison, or a sentence such as "imported
from Myne" is exactly what the Marks are for.

- the logo, wordmark and app icon under `public/` and `src/assets/`
- Open Graph and social images under `public/`
- screenshots and app renders in the user guide

Every one of those paths is covered by [`LICENSE-ASSETS`](LICENSE-ASSETS), not
by MIT or CC-BY-4.0.
