Once you have more than a handful of notes, organization starts to matter. Myne keeps the tools simple and local: folders to group notes, favorites for quick access, a trash you control, tabs and split view for working across notes, templates and daily notes for structure, and a sidebar you can arrange. This section covers each one.

## What's in this section

- **[Notes and folders](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/notes-and-folders/)**: creating, renaming, moving, and deleting notes and folders, and how autosave keeps up.
- **[Favorites](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/favorites/)**: pinning notes for quick access without moving them.
- **[Trash](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/trash/)**: how deletion works, and how to get a note back.
- **[Tabs and split view](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/tabs-and-split-view/)**: working across several notes at once.
- **[Templates](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/templates/)**: reusable note structures, with a small set of placeholders.
- **[Daily notes](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/daily-notes/)**: one note per day, on demand.
- **[Customizing the sidebar](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/sidebar-customization/)**: arranging the tree to match how you work.
