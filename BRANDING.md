# Brand assets

The Myne brand assets used by this site — the favicon (`public/favicon.svg`), the
`.github/banner.png`, and any logo/wordmark — are **mirrors** of the canonical brand
repository, [`myne-branding`](https://github.com/myne-md/myne-branding).

`myne-branding` is the single home for Myne's distributable brand assets (logo,
wordmark, app-icon exports, color/type sources, fonts, press imagery). This site keeps
its own copies because it bundles them at build time, but the **canonical source is
`myne-branding`** — when the brand changes there, refresh the copies here to match.

Brand marks are trademarks; their use is governed by the `myne-branding` repo's
[`TRADEMARK.md`](https://github.com/myne-md/myne-branding/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md).
