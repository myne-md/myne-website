Most of your time in Myne is spent in the editor, writing. This section covers the tools around the text: switching between Live preview, Reading, and Source view, focus mode for one paragraph at a time, folding long notes, editing with multiple cursors, find and replace, slash commands for quick inserts, comments alongside the text, spell check, the display settings that shape how the editor looks, saved views you can return to, and exporting a note to a plain file. Each one has its own article below.

## What's in this section

- **[View modes](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/view-modes/)**: switching between Live preview, Reading, and Source view, and what each shows.
- **[Focus mode](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/focus-mode/)**: dimming everything but the line or paragraph you are writing.
- **[Folding](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/folding/)**: collapsing headings and lists to move through a long note.
- **[Multiple cursors](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/multiple-cursors/)**: editing several places at once.
- **[Find and replace](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/find-and-replace/)**: searching within the current note and swapping text.
- **[Slash commands](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/slash-commands/)**: inserting structure by typing a slash.
- **[Comments](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/comments/)**: notes to yourself alongside the text.
- **[Document statistics](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/document-statistics/)**: word and character counts for the note, with a tooltip breakdown.
- **[Editor display](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/editor-display/)**: line width, font, and the other settings that shape the writing surface.
- **[Spell check](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/spell-check/)**: catching typos as you type.
- **[Saved views](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/saved-views/)**: returning to a layout you set up before.
- **[Exporting notes](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/export-notes/)**: writing a single note or your whole vault out to plain files.
