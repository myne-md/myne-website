Settings is where you adjust how Myne works, and this section also explains the privacy model the whole app is built on. This page is the tour; the rest of the section goes deeper.

## Opening settings

With a vault open, open Settings from the gear in the bottom bar or with `⌘,`. It opens over the editor, with five panels:

- **Account** holds your account number readout, the Change password action, and the Delete vault action.
- **Privacy** holds auto-lock and screen-capture protection.
- **Appearance** holds the light/dark/system theme and related display options.
- **Storage** covers backing up, restoring, and importing (the flows live in [Back up your vault](/user-guide/safety-and-recovery/back-up-your-vault/), [Restore from a backup](/user-guide/safety-and-recovery/restore-from-a-backup/), and [Import from Obsidian](/user-guide/attachments-and-import/import-from-obsidian/)).
- **About** holds version information and a changelog.

A few controls are shown but not yet active, such as a custom-theme option and the changelog. These are real, tracked features that will switch on when they land; they are not placeholders for things that may never come.

## Closing settings

Close settings with `Esc`, by clicking outside it, or with its close button. There is one exception: while a password change is being processed, settings won't close until the operation finishes. See [Change your password](/user-guide/settings-and-privacy/change-your-password/).
