Once you have more than a handful of notes, organization starts to matter. Myne keeps the tools simple and local: folders to group notes, favorites for quick access, a trash you control, an archive for notes you want out of the way but not gone, tabs and split view for working across notes, named workspaces to save layouts, templates and daily notes for structure, bulk actions for tidying many notes at once, and a sidebar you can arrange. This section covers each one.

## What's in this section

- **[Notes and folders](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/notes-and-folders/)**: creating, renaming, moving, and deleting notes and folders, and how autosave keeps up.
- **[Folder notes](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/folder-notes/)**: designating one note as a folder's index note, reachable from the folder row and from a trailing-slash wikilink.
- **[Favorites](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/favorites/)**: pinning notes for quick access without moving them.
- **[Trash](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/trash/)**: how deletion works, and how to get a note back.
- **[Archive](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/archive/)**: setting a note aside without deleting it — out of the sidebar, still searchable and linkable, kept indefinitely.
- **[Selecting and acting on multiple notes](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/bulk-note-actions/)**: selecting several notes in the sidebar to move, archive, or delete them together, plus duplicating and opening a random note.
- **[Tabs and split view](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/tabs-and-split-view/)**: working across several notes at once.
- **[Pinned and stacked tabs](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/pinned-and-stacked-tabs/)**: pinning a tab so navigation leaves it alone, and switching a pane to a stacked deck.
- **[Named workspaces](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/named-workspaces/)**: saving the current editor layout under a name and switching between layouts.
- **[Templates](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/templates/)**: reusable note structures, with a small set of placeholders.
- **[Daily notes](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/daily-notes/)**: one note per day, on demand.
- **[Saved views](/user-guide/editor-and-writing/saved-views/)**: filtered, projected card galleries of your notes in the Views sidebar section.
- **[Customizing the sidebar](/user-guide/notes-and-organization/sidebar-customization/)**: arranging the tree to match how you work.
